Native audio helper dependencies (not used by normal Cargo or Android builds)

nlohmann/json 3.11.3 is vendored under ../vendor/nlohmann.
Copyright (c) 2013-2023 Niels Lohmann.
Its full MIT license is in ../vendor/nlohmann/LICENSE.MIT.
Exact upstream URLs and SHA256 digests are in provenance.json in that directory.

The optional live host dynamically links system JACK (LGPL-2.1-or-later)
and FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1-or-later). The protocol layer dynamically links
system OpenSSL libcrypto (OpenSSL 3 is Apache-2.0; older versions have their
own terms). No copies of those libraries or their development headers are
redistributed in this source tree. Consult the exact system distribution
copyright files and upstream licenses when packaging the helper.

Upstreams:
https://jackaudio.org/
https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth
https://openssl-library.org/
https://github.com/nlohmann/json

No VST3 SDK, yabridge, Wine, SoundFonts or third-party plugins are bundled.
Optional Debian packages include this notice, dependencies.json and the
nlohmann license/provenance under /usr/share/doc/rustymusicsheets/audio-host/.
Runtime shared libraries are provided separately by the distribution and
listed in the package's generated Depends field, not bundled into the helper.
